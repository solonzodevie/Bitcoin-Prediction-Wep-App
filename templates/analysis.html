Bottom line
  • Direction is a coin flip. Walk-forward tests of Ridge & RandomForest never beat ~50% sign accuracy on next-day returns.
  • Magnitude is predictable. vol30, |ret1| and vol_rel explain ~8% of next-day |return| variance — small but real.
  • Volatility clusters. Large moves follow large moves; today's |return| predicts tomorrow's |return|.
  • Fat tails. A normal 90% band under-covers; widen confidence ranges in practice.
